Questions

Short answers.

Is LifeCheck free?

Yes. The free plan covers 5 life areas, 3 habits and two task packs, with no time limit. Pro unlocks the rest.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, with widgets, a Lock Screen widget, a Live Activity and an Apple Watch app. Android isn't available yet.

Is it a habit tracker?

It includes daily habits, but most of LifeCheck is the less frequent stuff: the yearly, monthly and seasonal tasks that keep a home, car, body and budget in good shape.

What happens to my data?

Read the privacy policy. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

When does it launch?

Soon. Leave your email and you'll get one message the day it's on the App Store.
