Manage your account & data

Account deletion, data export, and sign-in options live in the app under Settings → Account. Deleting your account removes your data from our servers immediately.

Subscriptions & purchases

LifeCheck Pro is billed through your Apple ID. To change or cancel, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases in the app (Settings or the upgrade screen) after reinstalling or switching devices.

Notifications & streaks

Reminder times, quiet hours, and weekly review settings are in Settings → Notifications. Streak protection (grace days, freeze tokens, vacation mode) is in Settings → Streak.
