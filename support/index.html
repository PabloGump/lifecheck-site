Quick answers

Most things live in the app's Settings. Tap your profile picture on Home to open them.

manage_accounts

Your account and data

Delete your account and its data in Settings›Account›Delete Account.

Want a copy first? Settings›Health & data›Export Records.

workspace_premium

Subscriptions

Pro is billed through your Apple ID. Change or cancel it in Settings›Premium›Manage subscription.

New phone or reinstall? Tap Settings›Premium›Restore purchases.

notifications

Reminders and notifications

Quiet hours, streak alerts and the Sunday-evening weekly recap are all in Settings›Notifications.

Not getting any? Check that notifications are allowed for LifeCheck in your iPhone's Settings too.

local_fire_department

Streaks

Grace days and streak freezes cover the odd missed day automatically.

Going away? Turn on Settings›Tools›Vacation pause so your streak waits for you.

widgets

Widgets and Apple Watch

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose LifeCheck.

On Apple Watch, install LifeCheck from the Watch app on your iPhone.

favorite

Apple Health

Connect it in Settings›Health & data›Apple Health and your workouts complete fitness tasks on their own (Pro).

Health data stays on your device.

Privacy questions? Read how we handle your data, or email us with the subject line "Privacy Request".
